Privacy Policy

How StepReward: Walk & Rewards collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated · September 8, 2026

Introduction & Scope

RH Studio PK ("we," "us," "our," or "the developer") is an independent developer based in Pakistan that publishes and operates the mobile application StepReward: Walk & Rewards (the "App," package identifier com.rhstudio.stepreward). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use and share that information, and the choices and rights available to you.

The App is made available to users worldwide. Some features — such as which cash-out or redemption destinations are shown to you — may vary depending on the country or region you select, but the core App and this Privacy Policy apply to all users regardless of location.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in to the App, you do so using a Google account through Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In. This process shares the following information from your Google account with the App: your name, email address, and profile photo. We use this information solely to create and manage your in-app account.

Profile Information

During onboarding, we ask you to provide your date of birth and your country or region. Your country/region is used to determine which cash-out or redemption destinations are relevant and available to you. We also automatically detect your device's timezone to support features such as daily check-ins and streaks.

Step & Activity Data

The App reads step-count data from your device's built-in step sensor and/or, where applicable, the Android Health Connect platform, which requires activity-recognition permission. A background/foreground tracking service (which may continue running after a device restart) records your step activity so that steps can be converted into in-app points. See the Health & Fitness Data section below for more detail on how this data is used and your rights over it.

Rewards, Transaction & Redemption Data

We collect and store data related to your in-app economy, including points, coins, scratch-card outcomes, virtual cash balance, and your redemption/payout history. When you request a redemption, you submit personal payout details directly into the App — for example, an email address linked to a payment account, or a phone number, wallet name, and account holder name for local mobile-wallet top-ups (such as JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or SadaPay). We collect and retain these payout details in order to process your request.

Referral Data

We generate a personal referral code associated with your account and track which users you have referred, so that referral rewards can be correctly credited.

Device, Usage & Advertising Data

We and our third-party advertising partner collect certain device and usage information, including an advertising/device identifier, general app-usage and interaction data, and technical information (such as device model and operating system version), for the purpose of delivering optional rewarded ads and occasional app-open ads, and, where applicable, ad personalization.

Notification Data

If you grant notification permission, the App may send you local notifications, such as streak reminders. We do not need to collect additional personal information beyond the permission itself to deliver these local notifications.

Health & Fitness Data

Step and activity data collected by the App is used exclusively to calculate the in-app rewards (points and coins) you earn from walking. It is not used, analyzed, or repurposed for any medical, diagnostic, or health-monitoring purpose.

We do not sell your step or activity data. We do not share this data with insurers, employers, healthcare providers, or any third party for purposes unrelated to operating the App's reward system. The App and its underlying sensors are not a medical device and are not intended to diagnose, treat, monitor, or manage any medical condition.

You may stop the collection of step and activity data at any time by revoking the App's activity-recognition or Health Connect permission through your device settings, or by disabling background tracking within the App, though doing so will prevent you from earning further step-based rewards. You may also request deletion of your historical step and activity data by contacting us using the details in the Contact Us section.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create, authenticate, and maintain your App account.
  • To calculate step-based points and coins, and to operate check-ins, scratch cards, and other reward mechanics.
  • To process and review redemption/payout requests, including verifying the details you submit.

  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms and Conditions.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App's functionality and performance.
  • To communicate with you regarding your account, support requests, or material changes to our policies.

Third-Party Services We Use

The App relies on a small number of third-party service providers to function:

  • Authentication & Cloud Backend Provider: We use a cloud-based authentication and database backend (a Firebase/Firestore-style service) to sign you in and to store your profile and reward data securely.
  • Advertising SDK: The App integrates a third-party mobile advertising SDK (such as Google AdMob) to deliver optional rewarded ads and app-open ads. This SDK may collect an advertising/device identifier for ad delivery and, where permitted, ad personalization.
  • Device Activity Platform: Where supported, the App reads step data through Android's Health Connect platform, which is governed by its own permission controls on your device.

These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually or technically restricted from using your information for purposes unrelated to providing their service to us, except as described in this Policy. We do not control the independent privacy practices of the app marketplace you downloaded this app from; please review that marketplace's own privacy terms separately.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers (such as our cloud backend and advertising SDK provider) who process data on our behalf strictly to operate the App.

  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of business assets, subject to continued protection of your data under a policy at least as protective as this one.
