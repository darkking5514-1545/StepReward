Description of Service

StepReward is a step-counter and walk-tracking rewards application, made available to users worldwide. The App reads your step activity from your device's step sensor and/or Android's Health Connect platform and converts eligible steps, daily check-ins, and admin-created step challenges into in-app points and coins. Points can also be earned by watching optional rewarded ads, referring other users, and completing optional "Earn" tasks (such as trying another app). Points may be converted to a real virtual cash balance only at fixed, pre-set conversion rates — this conversion does not involve chance or randomness. Your cash balance may be redeemed, subject to manual review, as a gift-card code delivered to an email address you provide. The App also displays optional advertising.
